About Biotech Park, Lucknow

India's Leading Biotechnology Hub & Scientific Incubator

Our Heritage

Empowering Science, Innovation & Enterprise

The Biotechnology Park in Lucknow, "The City of Nawabs," has transformed Lucknow into a leading Biotechnology City in North India. Established in 2002 through a partnership between the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Government of India, and the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of Uttar Pradesh, it became fully operational in 2007.

Registered under the Indian Societies Act of 1860, the park operates under a robust governance framework ensuring efficiency, transparency, and accountability. Located strategically in Lucknow, the park is a hub for biotechnology-driven enterprises, catalyzing advancements in agriculture, healthcare, and industrial applications.
