About Biotech Park

Pioneering Scientific Excellence in India

Biotech Park Lucknow is India's top research center, established under a partnership between the Department of Biotechnology (DBT), Govt. of India, and DST Govt. of Uttar Pradesh. We foster cutting-edge research in genomics, bioinformatics, molecular biology, and agricultural biotechnology.
