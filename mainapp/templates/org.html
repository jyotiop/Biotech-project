About The Organization

The Biotechnology Park in Lucknow, "The City of Nawabs," has transformed Lucknow into a leading Biotechnology City in North India. Established in 2002 through a partnership between DBT Govt. of India and DST Govt. of Uttar Pradesh, it operates under the Indian Societies Act of 1860.

It catalyzes advancements in agriculture, healthcare, and industrial applications through cutting-edge facilities, promoting academic-industry collaboration, startup incubation, and skill development across Northern India.

Our Vision

To improve the biotechnology landscape by providing infrastructure for R&D, product development, entrepreneurship, and translating lab-scale technologies to pilot-scale production in order to accomplish the vision of Aatmanirbhar Bharat and Viksit Bharat@2047.

Our Mission

  • Bridge the gap between technological advancements and biotech enterprises.
  • Promote and support startups, incubation, and entrepreneurship.
  • Provide functional links between knowledge epicenters, research labs, and universities in India and abroad.
